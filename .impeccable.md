## Design Context

### Users
Club leaders ordering supplies and rewards for their clubs. They need to scan a large catalog quickly, understand the real-world value of each item, and make confident ordering decisions from a dashboard.

### Brand Personality
Playful, welcoming, maker-minded. The interface should feel energetic and distinctly Hack Club without sacrificing clarity for practical ordering.

### Aesthetic Direction
Playful Hack Club catalog with tactile product imagery, warm color accents, and crisp editorial hierarchy. Support both light and dark dashboard themes. Avoid generic enterprise commerce UI, noisy gradients, and decorative elements that compete with product information.

### Design Principles
- Make the item photo and price the fastest things to understand.
- Show Hack Club Coins and USD together wherever price matters.
- Use friendly, concise copy that helps a club leader decide.
- Make details feel like a useful product page, not a disconnected popup.
- Keep contrast, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior accessible in both themes.
